1

Neutral VIN guidance

The customer-facing “VIN returned a warning” message and amber decoder warning have been removed. Visitors see a direct instruction:

“Review your vehicle details below, then continue.”

If lookup cannot supply the details, the form says:

“Enter your vehicle details below to continue.”

Year, make, and model remain editable. Spanish uses the same clear guidance.

2

Full pickup address

The quick form now collects the vehicle’s complete location alongside contact and screening details.

Required
Street address · City · State (CA) · Service-area ZIP
Optional
Apartment, suite, unit, or space

Address fields support browser autofill. California is prefilled; the pickup ZIP is checked against the service area.

Included in the inquiry email: pickup street address, optional unit/space, city, state, and ZIP, along with the vehicle, screening answers, and contact details.
